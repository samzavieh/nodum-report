This year NODUM did something most enterprise software companies have not done in a decade: it made the invisible visible. Not through abstraction. Not through metaphor. Through direct, diagrammatic evidence of the mechanisms that run modern organizations.

If you cannot see the system, you cannot trust the outcome.

The Underneath campaign was not a marketing exercise. It was a proof of concept — that systems intelligence, when communicated with the precision of the systems it describes, changes the way organizations make decisions. Twelve films. Six surfaces. One argument.

We deployed across seven US markets simultaneously. We placed in the Wall Street Journal, the Financial Times, and Wired. We do not believe in aspiration without mechanism.

Revenue grew 47% year-over-year. Enterprise contract value increased by $18M. The film series generated 2.3M verified impressions in the first ninety days without a single paid social placement. The system did the work. It always does.

Amir Nouri, Chief Executive Officer  ·  January 2025
